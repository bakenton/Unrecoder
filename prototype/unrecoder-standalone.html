CODE TABLE
How to read
      SECRET
      STATION HQ — CIPHER ROOM

      You are the new cipher clerk. Agents out in the city radio in secret messages. You are the one who can read them.

      1. Decode. A message arrives as a field of random letters. Hidden in it are the pieces of one sentence. Find them, and turn them into words with the code table and today's key.
      2. Read with care. Sometimes one code means two things. The agents' cards and the map on the wall tell you which one the agent meant.
      3. Reply. At the end of the shift command gives you an order. You encode it and send it back.

      A wrong guess only costs time. A wrong reading costs an agent. Mr. Pike, head of the room, will show you around.

      MR. PIKE head of the cipher room
