    TODAY'S KEY
    1. The signal is groups of three letters. The first letter is always Z: a mask, it means nothing.
    2. The second letter gives the row: find it in the vertical key, the digit it sits next to is the row.
    3. The third letter gives the column: same, in the horizontal key.
    4. Click that row and column in the table. The cell is a word, a letter, or a marker, and the sentence fills in. Each blank has a colour: look for table cells of that colour.
