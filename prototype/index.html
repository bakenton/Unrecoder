    HOW TO READ A TRANSMISSION
    1. Find a key in the noise: a digit touching a letter a–j (side by side or one above the other, either order). Click one, then the other.
    2. Look it up in the table: row = the digit, column = the letter. The cell is a word, a letter, or a marker.
    3. Fill the sentence in order. The message reads left to right, top to bottom, and each blank has a colour: look for table cells of that colour.
    4. TRY checks your keys (Likeness: how many are right, not which).
