    TODAY'S KEY
    1. Find the groups. A group is the letter Z and the two letters after it. Click a Z to mark it, click a marked group to unmark it. Careful: some Z groups are decoys. The real ones read as a sentence, in order.
    2. Ignore the Z. The second letter gives the row (vertical key above), the third gives the column (horizontal key).
    3. Click that cell in the table. The word fills its blank; each blank has a colour. A word that does not fit its blank means a decoy: unmark it.
    4. A ? is a lost letter: the other letter still gives a row or a column. Pick the cell of the right colour in it.
