THREE WAYS TO GET THE GROUPS OUT

1 · GRILLE

Slide and turn the card over the signal (buttons or arrow keys, R to turn). In the right spot every slot shows a Z and two letters.

2 · TELETYPE TAPE

The signal runs past. Click a group while it goes by. Missed ones come round again.

3 · NO Z

No Z, and extra letters thrown in. A group is two letters side by side: a ROW-key letter, then a COLUMN-key letter. Click its first letter.
